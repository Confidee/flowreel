# Code of Conduct

flowreel adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, as its code of
conduct:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

In short: we want flowreel's community to be open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive and healthy. Be kind and respectful, give and accept
constructive feedback gracefully, take responsibility for mistakes, and
focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, personal or
political attacks, sexualised language or imagery, publishing others'
private information, and any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a
professional setting are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in every flowreel community space (the
repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and any chat or
event space), and when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to **support@confidee.com**, with
"flowreel conduct" in the subject. The address reaches the project's
maintainers; if your report concerns one of them, say so in the subject and
it is handled by the others.

We acknowledge every report within 3 business days, review and investigate
it promptly and fairly, and tell you the outcome. We respect the reporter's
privacy: we do not share your identity or the details of the report
without your consent, beyond what investigating it requires.

Do not report a security vulnerability here: see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainers enforce this code of conduct following the Contributor
Covenant's enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban and
permanent ban, depending on the impact of the behaviour.
